My Story

How I Got Here

From childhood doodles to a full-time artist — here's how I found my way to frogs and illustrations.

The Beginning

Always Drawing

I was that kid who drew on everything. My mom still has notebooks from elementary school filled with dragons, animals, and made-up landscapes. A middle school art teacher named Mrs. Chen encouraged me to take my passion seriously, and that changed everything.

Finding My Style

Watercolor & Frogs

Throughout college, I explored different mediums — charcoal, acrylics, digital — but nothing clicked like watercolor. I fell in love with the way colors could blend and flow. The frog obsession started as a joke but became my signature, appearing in nearly every piece I create.

Today

Living the Dream

I've been a full-time artist for the past five years, and every day feels like a gift. My work has been featured in galleries and online spaces, and I love connecting with people who appreciate the beauty in nature and quirky amphibians. This is just the beginning.
