About Me

Get to Know Me

I'm a visual artist and illustrator based in Minnesota When I'm not creating art, you'll find me playing with my cats or obsessing over frogs, plants, and the natural world.

The Basics

My Journey to Art

I grew up doodling in the margins of my notebooks, but it wasn't until my teens that I realized I wanted to pursue art seriously. I studied illustration in college and have been creating full-time for the past five years, specializing in watercolor and ink work.

My Studio

Where the Magic Happens

My little studio in Portland is filled with paint, pencils, and inspiration. I work primarily in watercolor and ink, sometimes combining them with digital tools. A typical day involves sketching, painting, and of course, hiding frogs in my artwork.

Fun Facts

Beyond the Canvas

I have a collection of over 50 frog figurines and I've visited every major wetland in Oregon. My favorite hobbies include hiking, bookbinding, and hosting small artist gatherings in my studio. I believe nature is the greatest teacher.
