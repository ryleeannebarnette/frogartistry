About Me

Get to Know Me

I'm a visual artist and illustrator based in Portland, Oregon. When I'm not creating art, you'll find me exploring the Pacific Northwest or obsessing over different frog species.

The Basics

My Journey to Art

I grew up doodling in the margins of my notebooks, but it wasn't until my teens that I realized I wanted to pursue art seriously. I studied illustration in college and have been creating full-time ever since, specializing in detailed natural subjects and whimsical character design.

My Studio

Where the Magic Happens

My little studio in Portland is filled with paint, pencils, and inspiration. I work primarily in watercolor and ink, sometimes combining them with digital tools. A typical day involves sketching, color mixing, and lots of tea. There are always reference photos of frogs pinned to my walls!

Fun Facts

Beyond the Canvas

I have a collection of over 50 frog figurines and I've visited every major wetland in Oregon. My favorite hobbies include hiking, bookbinding, and hosting small artist gatherings in my studio. Fun fact: my cat is named Tadpole!
